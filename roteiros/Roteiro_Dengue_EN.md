# Script — Animation Video: Dengue and Stick Figures

**Project:** Science is Wonderful! 2026 — Brussels  
**Booth #18** — Health detectives: how diseases spread through insects  
**Format:** Stick figure animation (white background, black stick figures, emoji objects)  
**Estimated duration:** 2 minutes  
**Narration:** Soft female voice in English

---

## Scene 1 — What is dengue? (0:00 – 0:25)

**Visual description:** White background with light blue sky gradient. The Narrator 
(stick figure with white lab coat and glasses) appears on the left. On the right, an 
animated sequence shows the life cycle of *Aedes aegypti*: egg, larva, pupa, and adult 
mosquito, with arrows indicating progression. Next to it, the *Aedes albopictus* (tiger 
mosquito) appears with visible white stripes. At the bottom, a container with standing 
water and eggs being deposited.

**Narration:** "This is the *Aedes aegypti*, it transmits the dengue virus. The *Aedes albopictus*, 
better known as the tiger mosquito, can also transmit dengue. Female *Aedes* lay their eggs 
in standing water. An egg takes between 7 and 10 days to become an adult mosquito."

---

## Scene 2 — No control measures (0:25 – 0:55)

**Visual description:** A village with 4 colourful houses and 8 residents (stick figures). 
Mosquitoes fly freely between them, biting residents. Those bitten show floating symptoms: 
🤒 (fever), 🤕 (headache), red spots. Counter in the upper right corner: Bitten 8/8, 
Infected 6/8 (red bar).

**Narration:** "Without any control measures, dengue is passed to humans through mosquito 
bites. Only females bite humans, as they need blood for their eggs to develop. Dengue 
symptoms include high fever, intense headache, body aches, and red spots on the skin."

| Indicator | Value |
|---|---|
| Bitten | 8 of 8 |
| Infected | 6 of 8 |

---

## Scene 3 — Individual control (0:55 – 1:20)

**Visual description:** Same village, but now some residents use individual protection 
measures: repellent (blue bottle), long clothing (long sleeves and trousers), and 
mosquito nets over beds. Mosquitoes try to bite but are blocked by blue shields on 
protected residents. Counter: Bitten 5/8, Infected 3/8 (orange bar).

**Narration:** "With individual measures, such as repellent, long clothing, and mosquito 
nets, some people can protect themselves. But other measures can help too."

| Indicator | Value |
|---|---|
| Bitten | 5 of 8 |
| Infected | 3 of 8 |

---

## Scene 4 — Collective control (1:20 – 1:50)

**Visual description:** The village now has collective measures. Some residents 
receive vaccines (💉), gaining golden shields of protection. A fumigation truck 
drives through the street, eliminating mosquitoes with an insecticide cloud. The 
remaining mosquitoes visibly decrease. Counter: Bitten 4/8, Infected 1/8 (yellow bar).

**Narration:** "Collective measures make a big difference! Vaccines, when available, 
protect people even when bitten. Chemical interventions that are safe for the environment, 
can reduce the mosquito population. The combination of different measures strengthens 
the fight against the disease."

| Indicator | Value |
|---|---|
| Bitten | 4 of 8 |
| Infected | 1 of 8 |

---

## Scene 5 — Environmental control (1:50 – 2:20)

**Visual description:** Residents carry out a clean-up effort. Stick figures collect 
rubbish (🗑️), cover water containers, turn over tyres and bottles. Breeding sites
progressively disappear. Mosquitoes vanish from the scene. Clean and organised 
environment. Counter: Bitten 1/8, Infected 0/8 (green bar).

**Narration:** "When we take care of the environment, removing rubbish, covering water 
containers, and eliminating breeding sites, mosquitoes lose them 
and have no place to lay their eggs and develop into adults!"

| Indicator | Value |
|---|---|
| Bitten | 1 of 8 |
| Infected | 0 of 8 |

---

## Scene 6 — Conditions that change everything (2:20 – 2:40)

**Visual description:** Screen divided into 4 quadrants, each showing a different 
environmental condition:

| Quadrant | Condition | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Upper left | 🌧️ Rainy season | +1 mosquito appears |
| Upper right | 🗑️ Accumulated rubbish | +1 mosquito hatches from breeding site |
| Lower left | 🧹 Clean-up effort | -1 mosquito disappears |
| Lower right | 🏙️ Dense urban area | Mosquitoes bite more easily |

**Narration:** "The environment makes all the difference. Rain and discarded waste 
together produce more breeding sites. Cleaning and care eliminates them. In dense 
urban areas, the risk is even greater."

---

## Scene 7 — Closing and call to action (2:40 – 3:00)

**Visual description:** Circle of 8 stick figures holding hands in the centre of 
the screen. Animated text appears: "Dengue: know to prevent." Below: "Have you also 
thought about researching other diseases like malaria, leishmaniasis and Chagas?" 
Footer: "Science is Wonderful! 2026 — Brussels".

**Narration:** "The same mosquitoes that transmit dengue also transmit Zika and chikungunya. 
But with knowledge and collective action, we can win this battle. Protect yourself. 
Protect your community. Take care of the environment."
